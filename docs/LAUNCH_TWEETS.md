**Tweet 1 — what it is / why Midnight**

Most "age gates" and KYC checks make you hand over way more than a
yes/no answer.

Veilcred proves your credential clears a bar — 18+, KYC tier 2, active
license — and puts only that boolean on-chain. Built on @MidnightNtwrk,
where privacy is the default, not an add-on.

---

**Tweet 2 — technical insight on the privacy model**

The interesting part isn't hiding data — it's the nullifier.

Same credential, same gate, twice → rejected as a replay.
Same credential, different gate → completely unlinkable nullifier.

One value lets you prevent reuse *and* preserve privacy. `disclose()` in
Compact makes exactly two things public: the boolean, and that hash.
Everything else — issuer, expiry, the real value — never leaves your
device.
